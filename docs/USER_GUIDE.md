## 1. What is WasteFi?

### 1.1 Overview

WasteFi connects waste collectors with collection points and rewards proper waste management with WASTE tokens. Our platform:

✅ **Rewards recycling**: Earn tokens for every kilogram of waste collected  
✅ **Tracks your impact**: See how much waste you've diverted from landfills  
✅ **Builds reputation**: Higher reputation = better rewards  
✅ **100% transparent**: All transactions recorded on blockchain  
✅ **Secure payments**: Automatic payment after verification  

---

### 1.2 How It Works

```
1. REGISTER → 2. COLLECT → 3. SUBMIT → 4. VERIFY → 5. GET PAID
```

1. **Register** as a collector on the platform
2. **Collect** recyclable waste (plastic, paper, metal, glass, organic)
3. **Submit** your collection at a verified collection point
4. **Verify** - Collection point verifies weight and material
5. **Get Paid** - Receive WASTE tokens automatically

---

### 1.3 Supported Materials

| Material | Examples | Current Rate* |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Plastic** | Bottles, containers, bags | ~0.012 WASTE/kg |
| **Paper** | Newspapers, cardboard, office paper | ~0.0085 WASTE/kg |
| **Metal** | Cans, aluminum foil, scrap metal | ~0.016 WASTE/kg |
| **Glass** | Bottles, jars, broken glass | ~0.0065 WASTE/kg |
| **Organic** | Food waste, garden waste | ~0.0045 WASTE/kg |

*Rates updated weekly based on market conditions

## 3. How to Submit Waste Collections

### 3.1 Finding Collection Points

**In the App**:
1. Open WasteFi app
2. Tap "Find Collection Points"
3. Allow location access (or enter address manually)
4. View map of nearby collection points
5. Tap on a point to see:
   - Address and operating hours
   - Materials accepted
   - Current wait time
   - User ratings

**Tips for choosing collection points**:
- ✅ Choose verified points (green checkmark)
- ✅ Check accepted materials before visiting
- ✅ Note operating hours
- ✅ Call ahead for large collections

---

### 3.2 Submitting a Collection

#### Option 1: In-Person at Collection Point

1. **Bring your waste** to a collection point
2. **Separate materials** by type (plastic, paper, metal, etc.)
3. **Weigh your materials** (collection point has scales)
4. **Open WasteFi app** → Tap "Submit Collection"
5. **Scan collection point QR code** (or select from list)
6. **Select material type**
7. **Enter weight** (kg)
8. **Tap "Submit"**
9. **Get receipt** with transaction ID

**⏱️ Transaction submitted! Awaiting verification...**

---

#### Option 2: Bulk/Scheduled Collection

For larger quantities:
1. In app, tap "Schedule Bulk Collection"
2. Select materials and estimated quantities
3. Choose collection point
4. Select pickup date/time (if available)
5. Submit request
6. Collection point confirms appointment
7. On collection day, follow normal submission process

---

### 3.3 What Happens After Submission

**Step 1: Verification (1-24 hours)**
- Collection point verifies:
  - Material type is correct
  - Weight is accurate
  - Quality meets standards
- You receive notification when verified

**Step 2: Payment Processing (Automatic)**
- System calculates payment based on:
  - Material type
  - Weight
  - Current market price
  - Your reputation score (bonus!)
- Tokens automatically sent to your wallet

**Step 3: Confirmation**
- You receive payment confirmation
- Transaction appears in your history
- Reputation score updated

---

### 3.4 Transaction Status Explained

| Status | Meaning | What to Do |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Pending** | Awaiting verification | Wait for collection point to verify (usually within 24h) |
| **Verified** | Approved, payment processing | Payment will arrive shortly (usually within 1 hour) |
| **Paid** | Payment completed | Check your wallet! You've been paid 💰 |
| **Rejected** | Not verified | Contact collection point for reason |

## 4. Understanding Payments and Rewards

### 4.1 How Payment is Calculated

**Base Formula**:
```
Payment = Weight (kg) × Material Price (WASTE/kg)
```

**With Reputation Bonus**:
```
Final Payment = Base Payment × Reputation Multiplier
```

**Reputation Multipliers**:
- Excellent (800-1000): 1.2x (20% bonus!)
- Good (600-799): 1.1x (10% bonus)
- Fair (400-599): 1.0x (no bonus)
- Poor (200-399): 0.9x (10% penalty)
- Bad (0-199): 0.8x (20% penalty)

**Example**:
- Material: Plastic
- Weight: 5 kg
- Price: 0.012 WASTE/kg
- Your Reputation: 750 (Good → 1.1x multiplier)
- **Base Payment**: 5 × 0.012 = 0.06 WASTE
- **Final Payment**: 0.06 × 1.1 = **0.066 WASTE** ✨

## 6. Collection Point Guide

### 6.1 For Collection Point Operators

**Registration Process**:
1. Contact WasteFi admin (admin@wastefi.io)
2. Provide:
   - Business name and registration documents
   - Physical address
   - Operating hours
   - Materials you can accept
   - Scale certification (for weight verification)
3. Complete verification process
4. Receive collection point account
5. Get QR code for your location

---

### 6.2 Verifying Collections

**As a Collection Point**:
1. Collector brings waste to your point
2. Verify and weigh materials on certified scale
3. In your Collection Point app:
   - Scan collector's QR code or enter transaction ID
   - Confirm material type
   - Confirm weight
   - Tap "Verify" or "Reject"
4. If verified:
   - Collector receives notification
   - Payment processes automatically
5. If rejected:
   - Provide reason for rejection
   - Collector can appeal if needed

**Verification Standards**:
- ✅ Correct material type
- ✅ Clean, sorted materials
- ✅ Accurate weight (no water, contaminants)
- ✅ Meets quality standards
- ❌ Mixed/contaminated materials
- ❌ Weight discrepancies >5%

---

### 6.3 Best Practices

**For Collection Points**:
- Verify all collections within 24 hours
- Maintain calibrated scales
- Provide clear rejection reasons
- Be fair and consistent
- Keep facility clean and organized
- Post operating hours clearly

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

### 8.1 Registration & Account

**Q: Is there a fee to register?**  
A: No, registration is completely free!

**Q: How long does registration take?**  
A: Registration is instant if all information is provided correctly.

**Q: Can I register multiple accounts?**  
A: No, one account per person/phone number. Multiple accounts may be banned.

**Q: What if I forget my wallet password?**  
A: You'll need your 24-word recovery phrase to restore access. WasteFi cannot reset your wallet - this is blockchain security!

---

### 8.2 Collections & Payments

**Q: How long until my collection is verified?**  
A: Most collections are verified within 24 hours, often much faster.

**Q: What if my collection is rejected?**  
A: Check the rejection reason in your transaction history. Common reasons: incorrect material type, weight discrepancy, or quality issues. Contact the collection point for clarification.

**Q: When do I receive payment?**  
A: Automatically within 1 hour after verification, usually much faster.

**Q: What if I don't receive payment?**  
A: Check your transaction status. If status is "Paid" but you don't see tokens, check your wallet. If issue persists, contact support@wastefi.io

**Q: Can I cancel a submitted collection?**  
A: No, once submitted, collections cannot be cancelled. Only collection points can reject.

---

### 8.3 Reputation

**Q: Why did my reputation score decrease?**  
A: Check your score history for details. Common reasons: rejected submission, inactivity, or quality issues.

**Q: How can I improve my reputation faster?**  
A: Submit regular, high-quality collections. Accuracy and consistency are key!

**Q: Can my reputation go below 0?**  
A: No, reputation is clamped between 0 and 1000.

---

### 8.4 Tokens & Rewards

**Q: What can I do with WASTE tokens?**  
A: Currently, you can hold them as rewards, send to others, or use for platform services. Exchange listing coming soon!

**Q: Are there minimum amounts to earn?**  
A: No minimum for earning. Every kilogram counts!

**Q: Do tokens expire?**  
A: No, WASTE tokens never expire.

**Q: How do I check token price?**  
A: In-app: Wallet → Token Info. External exchanges will list price once trading begins.

---

### 8.5 Security

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes! Your personal data is encrypted. Transaction data is on public blockchain but doesn't reveal your identity.

**Q: What if someone steals my phone?**  
A: Your wallet is protected by your password/biometrics. If phone is stolen, restore your wallet on a new device using your 24-word recovery phrase.

**Q: Can WasteFi access my tokens?**  
A: No! Your wallet is non-custodial. Only you control your tokens.

## 9. Troubleshooting

### 9.1 Common Issues

#### "Transaction Failed" Error

**Possible Causes**:
- Network connectivity issue
- Collection point not verified
- You're not registered
- Rate limit exceeded (max 20/hour)

**Solutions**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify collection point is active
3. Confirm your registration status
4. Wait if you've submitted many transactions recently
5. Try again in a few minutes

---

#### "Collector Not Active" Error

**Causes**:
- Account suspended
- Account banned
- Registration pending

**Solutions**:
1. Check your account status in Profile
2. If suspended, contact support (may be temporary)
3. If banned, review Terms of Service violations
4. If pending, complete registration process

---

#### "Duplicate Transaction" Warning

**Cause**: You've recently submitted a very similar collection (same material, weight, location within 1 hour)

**Solutions**:
1. If it's truly a new collection, wait 1 hour and retry
2. If it's a mistake, you can ignore the previous submission
3. Contact collection point if you believe this is an error

---

#### App Won't Connect to Wallet

**Solutions**:
1. Make sure wallet browser extension/app is installed
2. Check that wallet is unlocked
3. Verify you're on the correct network (testnet vs mainnet)
4. Try disconnecting and reconnecting
5. Clear app cache and restart

---
